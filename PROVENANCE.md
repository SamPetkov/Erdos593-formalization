# Independent development and formal verification

This source accompanies Samuil Petkov's independently developed proof of the
classification of obligatory triple systems and its finite-structure extensions.
Eric Li's separate proof of the classification is cited in the manuscript.
Neither shared mathematical structure nor the order of public posting is used
here to infer informational dependence between the two developments.

The independent-development statement is the author's account. Kernel checking
verifies the stated formal mathematics; it does not adjudicate historical
priority, informational independence, or the role of private correspondence.
No private correspondence is included or invoked as public evidence here.

Classical results used in the mathematical development retain their original
attribution. A verified classification theorem does not, by itself, establish
that every later finite-spectrum, lattice, enumeration or stability extension
has been formally verified. RELEASE.json records the particular source scope.
